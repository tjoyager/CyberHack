# PITCH DECK NARRATIVE: CYBERHACK 2026 - SIMA AROME ERP LITE

## Slide 1: Title & Hook
**Headline:** Sima Arome ERP Lite: Digitizing the Scent of Success.
**Sub-headline:** Eliminating the "Double-Entry" bottleneck in the Flavor & Fragrance Industry.
**Visual Idea:** Background showing high-tech lab equipment mixed with natural extracts (Vanilla, Lavender).

## Slide 2: The Problem (The "Double-Input" Chaos)
**Story:** Sima Arome staff currently inputs data twice—once in manual logs and again in basic spreadsheets. 
**Pain Points:**
- **Human Error:** Manual entry leads to 15% data inconsistency in lot tracking.
- **QC Delays:** Ingredients sit in the warehouse for days because the QC team hasn't received the physical paper trail.
- **Audit Risks:** Hard to trace which staff approved a specific batch during a product recall.

## Slide 3: The Solution (Enterprise-Ready Automation)
**Headline:** A Single Source of Truth for Raw Materials.
**Value Proposition:**
- **Integrated Workflow:** One-time entry at Intake automatically triggers QC tasks and PPIC scheduling.
- **Immutable Logs:** Every status change is cryptographically linked to a user via JWT and recorded in Audit Logs.
- **RBAC Security:** Staff only see what they need to see. No accidental deletions, no unauthorized approvals.
